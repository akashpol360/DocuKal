DocuKal Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

DocuKal is an Android application designed to help you organize documents, warranty information, important dates, and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

DocuKal does not require you to create an account. Document details, notes, warranty information, and important dates that you enter into the app are stored locally on your Android device. DocuKal does not operate a cloud database for storing this application data.

2. Documents and Attachments

DocuKal uses Android's Storage Access Framework to let you select documents, invoices, and other files from your device. The app stores references to selected files so that you can access them again, subject to Android permissions and file availability.

Your selected attachments are not uploaded to a DocuKal server. If you move or delete a selected file, the corresponding reference in the app may no longer work.

3. Reminders and Notifications

DocuKal can schedule local reminders for important dates, warranty expirations, renewals, and other deadlines. Notifications depend on your device settings and any permissions required by Android.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of DocuKal may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and other data as required to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

The information collected may depend on your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and the advertising services used by the app.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about apps that use its services .

5. Premium Purchases

DocuKal offers an optional one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the purchase. DocuKal does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Premium users can use the app without its interstitial advertisements. The free version includes interstitial advertisements at certain save points.

For information about how Google handles purchase-related information, please review Google's applicable privacy policies and Google Play terms.

6. How Information Is Used

Information you enter into DocuKal is used to provide the app's document organization, warranty tracking, and reminder features. This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a DocuKal cloud database.

7. Data Sharing

DocuKal does not sell your locally stored document or warranty information. The app does not upload your selected attachments to a DocuKal server.

Third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google Play, may process information as necessary to provide advertising, purchases, security, and related services, subject to their applicable policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Application data is stored locally on your device. You can delete data using the app's available features or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it through Android settings.

Clearing storage or uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored information. Files and backup archives saved separately may need to be deleted separately.

9. Local Backups

DocuKal allows you to create local ZIP backups of application data and save them using your device's storage facilities. You control where these backup files are stored. Keep backup files secure because they may contain information you entered into the app.

10. Children's Privacy

DocuKal is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you have concerns about information processed by a third-party service used by the app, please consult that service's privacy policy.

11. Security

DocuKal is designed to keep your document information on your device rather than in a DocuKal cloud database. You should protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep exported backup files secure.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy URL provided on the DocuKal Google Play Store listing.
