Privacy Policy — DocuKal

Effective date: September 9, 2026

DocuKal ("the App") is a document, warranty, and important-date organizer. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information stored by DocuKal

DocuKal is designed to keep your document and warranty information on your Android device.

The App may store locally:

  • Document, warranty, and important-date metadata you enter.
  • References to attachments selected through Android's Storage Access Framework.
  • Local reminder and application settings needed for the App to function.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not use a DocuKal cloud database.

2. Attachments

When you select a document or invoice using Android's file picker, DocuKal stores a reference to the selected file and the permission needed to access it again.

Selected files are not uploaded by DocuKal to a DocuKal server or cloud database.

3. Advertising

The free version of DocuKal uses Google AdMob interstitial advertising at certain save points. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device, advertising, and usage information as described by Google.

Premium users do not receive the App's interstitial advertisements.

For information about Google's handling of data for advertising, please review Google's current privacy documentation and advertising policies.

4. Google Play purchases

Premium is offered as a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Payment and transaction processing are handled by Google Play. DocuKal does not collect or store your payment-card information.

5. Analytics and third-party services

DocuKal does not use Firebase, Supabase, an AI service, or a separate DocuKal analytics platform.

The App does use Google services required for:

  • AdMob advertising in the free version.
  • Google Play in-app purchase processing.

Those services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Notifications and reminders

If you allow notification permission, DocuKal may display local reminder notifications for dates and documents. These reminders are generated by the App on your device.

7. Backups

DocuKal can create local ZIP backups containing application metadata. Backups are created and handled through your device's storage facilities. DocuKal does not automatically upload backups to a DocuKal server.

8. Children's privacy

DocuKal is not intended to collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's data practices or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be included with future releases of the App.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning DocuKal, please contact the developer at akpolstudio@gmail.com.
